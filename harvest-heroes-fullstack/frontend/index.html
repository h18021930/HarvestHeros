📖 Farmer instructions

1. Create or choose a field

Open GAME to add a location, crop, soil, season, and water access. Starting a challenge saves your field. You can also pick coordinates in LOCATION MAP; exact GPS is optional.

2. Check real NASA weather history

In LOCATION MAP, choose coordinates and select NASA POWER HISTORY, or use DASHBOARD to fetch data for a saved field. The values are historical gridded air temperature and precipitation estimates, not real-time readings, live soil moisture, forecasts, or field-level diagnoses. If NASA is unavailable, the app displays an error rather than inventing measurements.

3. Keep farming records

Use TRACKER to record planting dates and care activities. Timeline and harvest dates are rough estimates. Use ROTATION to compare crop families and optionally request historical climate-based rotation rankings. Check local soil tests and professional agricultural guidance before acting on recommendations.

4. Play and keep your rewards

GAME keeps the original pixel battles, XP, levels, monster journal, and unlocked field plans. SAVED keeps field plans and your private access code. Your progress is stored in the server database. Copy your access code to restore your profile on another device and keep it secret.

5. Start again when you want

In SAVED, use RESET GAME PROGRESS to clear your XP, victories, battles, game journal, and unlocked plans while keeping your fields, plantings, and activity logs. Alternatively choose RESET EVERYTHING for this profile, including farming records. Both require confirmation and cannot be undone. These actions do not affect other profiles.
